## 🔹 Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

To synthesize the findings from the previous tasks into a clear business narrative and use statistical methods to validate a key business question.

### Key Activities

- Data storytelling
- Business-focused analysis
- Final presentation development
- Hypothesis formulation
- Statistical testing
- P-value interpretation
- Confidence interval analysis
- Business conclusion
- Business recommendations

### Hypothesis Testing

**Business Question:**

> Does average sales value differ significantly between male and female customers?

**Null Hypothesis (H₀):**  
There is no statistically significant difference in average sales between male and female customers.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):**  
There is a statistically significant difference in average sales between male and female customers.

### Statistical Test

An independent two-sample **Welch's t-test** was used with a significance level of **0.05**.

### Results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Male records | 497 |
| Female records | 476 |
| Male average sales | ₹141,438.90 |
| Female average sales | ₹137,181.66 |
| T-statistic | 0.5849 |
| P-value | 0.558718 |
| Degrees of freedom | 970.96 |
| 95% Confidence Interval | ₹−10,025.11 to ₹18,539.58 |

Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, the analysis did not find a statistically significant difference in average sales between the two groups.

### Deliverables

- Data storytelling analysis
- Final presentation deck
- Hypothesis testing summary
- Statistical interpretation
- Business recommendations
